About Me

Building Skills Through Experience, Education & Community Impact

Hello, I'm Jonathan (Jon) Boyen, a Business and Psychology student based in Melbourne, Australia. I am passionate about understanding people, developing practical solutions, and creating positive outcomes for organisations, communities, and individuals.


My professional journey began in customer-facing environments where I developed strong foundations in communication, teamwork, problem-solving, and leadership. Through roles with ALDI Australia, KFC South Pacific, and CGL Retail Services, I have gained experience working in fast-paced environments, supporting customers, training team members, maintaining operational standards, and contributing to business performance.


Alongside my professional experience, I am currently studying a Bachelor of Psychological Studies and Bachelor of Business (Marketing) at Victoria University. This combination of disciplines provides a unique perspective on both human behaviour and strategic decision-making, allowing me to better understand how people, organisations, and communities interact and grow.

Education
