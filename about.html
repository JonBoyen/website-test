My Story

Hello, I'm Jonathan (Jon) Boyen, a Business and Psychology student based in Melbourne, Australia, with a passion for understanding people, developing effective solutions, and creating positive outcomes within both organisations and communities.


My professional journey began in the fast-paced retail and hospitality industry, where I developed strong foundations in customer service, teamwork, leadership, and operational excellence. Through roles at ALDI Australia, KFC South Pacific, and CGL Retail Services, I have gained valuable experience working in customer-focused environments, training team members, maintaining high operational standards, and evaluating business performance.


Alongside my professional experience, I am currently undertaking a Bachelor of Psychological Studies and Bachelor of Business (Marketing) at Victoria University. This unique combination of disciplines allows me to explore both human behaviour and strategic decision-making, equipping me with skills that are valuable across business, marketing, community services, and people-focused professions.


Community involvement has always been important to me. Through my volunteer work with Blue Light Victoria and HeartKids, I have had the opportunity to support young people, contribute to community programs, and assist with fundraising and engagement initiatives that make a meaningful difference in people's lives.


I am particularly interested in leadership, marketing, community engagement, counselling pathways, organisational behaviour, and the ways businesses can create positive social impact. My goal is to continue developing professionally while contributing to organisations and communities through strong communication, empathy, strategic thinking, and a commitment to continuous learning.
